# AI SUMMER SCHOOL 2026 Prompt Library

## Shared Style

Use this shared style for every generated image.

- Unified illustration system for AI SUMMER SCHOOL 2026 slides
- Worldbuilding: a group climbing the unknown mountain of generative AI together
- Bright, hopeful blend of mountain hike, camp, school trip, and summer school
- Friendly but polished flat illustration with light hand-drawn warmth
- Adult learners, mixed women and men, roughly 30s-50s
- Light outdoor items such as backpacks, hats, bottles, laptops, tablets, notebooks
- Palette centered on ivory, light blue, navy, mint green, summer yellow, pale orange
- White or pale ivory background
- Plenty of whitespace for slide layout
- Suitable for Japanese business seminar materials
- No text, no logo, no watermark, no brand name inside the image
- Wide 16:9-friendly composition
- High resolution

## Main Visual

### Cover: Start the Climb

Show participants starting to climb the mountain of generative AI.

- Summer sky and green mountain
- Around five adult participants, mixed women and men
- Devices such as laptop, tablet, phone, notebook
- Looking toward the summit with excitement
- Soft light at the summit to symbolize future, insight, and work potential
- Guide woman at the front
- Forward-looking composition with shared direction

## Avoid

Never include these traits.

- childish educational style
- too-anime character design
- photoreal imagery
- harsh survival mountaineering
- snow mountains, dangerous cliffs, fear, distress
- AI dominating humans
- only women or only young people
- stiff suit-only business imagery
- neon or heavy cyber styling
- overly dense or complicated diagrams
- Japanese text inside the image
- random English text
- company logos or service logos
- glossy 3D look
- dark background
- unnatural hands or fingers

- character illustration
